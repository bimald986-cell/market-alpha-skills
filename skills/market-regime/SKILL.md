# Market Regime Skill

Analyze current market conditions and recommend how much capital to allocate to stocks.

Focus on:
- Market breadth (participation)
- Trend of major indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq)
- Volatility and risk appetite
- Sector leadership (especially AI / semiconductors)

Output a clear regime: Bullish / Neutral / Cautious and a suggested exposure percentage.
